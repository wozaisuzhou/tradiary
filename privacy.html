Privacy Policy

Tradiary does not collect your data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Effective date: November 19, 2026 · Developer: Shunji Yan · Contact: yanshunji@gmail.com

  1. What we collect. Nothing. Tradiary has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads and no analytics or tracking tools. The developer never receives your trades, holdings or prices.
  2. What stays on your device. The purchases, sells, fees, notes and prices you enter are stored locally on your iPhone. They are not sent to the developer or to any server.
  3. Internet use. The app does not send your trades, holdings, notes or prices anywhere. The app has no account and no server. If paid options are added in the future, purchases will be handled by Apple through the App Store, and the developer will receive no payment details. Reminders are local notifications created on your device.
  4. Future price feeds. If a later version adds automatic price updates, the stock symbols you hold would be sent to the price provider you choose so it can return prices. Your API key would be stored on your device. We will update this policy before that feature ships.
  5. Backups. If you use iCloud Backup or an encrypted device backup, your device may include Tradiary data in it. That is handled by Apple under your Apple ID settings, not by the developer.
  6. Your control. You can edit or delete any record in the app. Deleting the app removes its data from your device.
  7. Children. Tradiary is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.
  8. Canadian privacy law. Because we do not collect personal information, we hold none to access or correct. If this changes, we will follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial law.
  9. Changes. We will post any change to this policy with a new effective date.
  10. Contact. Questions: yanshunji@gmail.com.

隐私政策

Tradiary 不收集你的数据。你输入的所有内容都只保存在你的设备上。

生效日期:November 19, 2026 · 开发者:Shunji Yan · 联系方式:yanshunji@gmail.com

  1. 我们收集什么。 什么都不收集。Tradiary 没有账号、不需要登录,没有广告,也没有统计分析或追踪工具。开发者不会收到你的交易、持仓或价格。
  2. 保存在你设备上的内容。 你输入的买入、卖出、手续费、备注和价格,都保存在你的 iPhone 本地,不会发送给开发者或任何服务器。
  3. 网络使用。 App 不会把你的交易、持仓、笔记或价格发送到任何地方。App 没有账号,也没有服务器。如果将来增加付费选项,购买将由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理,开发者不会收到任何支付信息。提醒是在你设备上创建的本地通知。
  4. 未来的行情功能。 如果以后的版本增加自动更新价格,你持有的股票代码会发送给你选择的行情提供商,以便获取价格;你的 API Key 会保存在你的设备上。该功能上线前,我们会先更新本政策。
  5. 备份。 如果你使用 iCloud 备份或加密的设备备份,你的设备备份可能包含 Tradiary 的数据。这由 Apple 按你的 Apple ID 设置处理,与开发者无关。
  6. 你的控制权。 你可以在 App 内编辑或删除任何记录。删除 App 会同时从你的设备上移除它的数据。
  7. 儿童。 Tradiary 不面向儿童,我们也不会有意收集任何人的信息。
  8. 加拿大隐私法。 由于我们不收集个人信息,因此没有可供查阅或更正的个人信息。如果以后发生变化,我们将遵守加拿大《个人信息保护与电子文件法》(PIPEDA)及适用的省级法律。
  9. 变更。 本政策有任何变更,我们会发布并注明新的生效日期。
  10. 联系方式。 如有疑问,请联系 yanshunji@gmail.com。
