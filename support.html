Support

Questions, a file that will not import, or something that looks wrong? Email yanshunji@gmail.com.

Where is my data?
Only on your iPhone. Tradiary has no account and no server. Use Settings (gear icon) → Data & backup → Back up all data, and save the file to iCloud Drive to keep a copy.
How do I move to a new phone?
Back up on the old phone, then on the new phone use Data & backup → Restore from backup.
Why do I enter prices myself?
Tradiary does not download market quotes. Open a stock and type the current price to see unrealized profit or loss.
My broker's CSV will not import.
Email the file (you may change the amounts) and say which broker it came from. Only US-dollar stock and ETF buys and sells are imported; options, short sales and dividends are skipped.
A stock split happened.
Open the stock → ⋯ menu → Stock split, and enter the ratio and date.

支持

有问题、文件导入不了,或者数字看起来不对?请发邮件到 yanshunji@gmail.com。

我的数据在哪里?
只在你的 iPhone 上。Tradiary 没有账号,也没有服务器。在 设置(齿轮图标)→ 数据与备份 → 备份全部数据,把文件存到 iCloud 云盘,就有一份副本。
怎么换到新手机?
在旧手机上备份,然后在新手机上用 数据与备份 → 从备份恢复。
为什么要自己输入价格?
Tradiary 不下载行情。打开某只股票,输入当前价格,就能看到浮动盈亏。
券商的 CSV 导入不了。
把文件发邮件给我们(金额可以改掉),并说明是哪家券商。只导入美元计价的股票和 ETF 买卖,期权、做空和分红会跳过。
股票拆股了。
打开这只股票 → 右上角 ⋯ → 拆股,填比例和日期。
